FAQ

How does remaining lease length change club value?

Under 5 remaining years compresses buyer multiples toward 2.5x–3.0x EBITDA or hardware salvage value. A 10–15 year lease unlocks 4.0x–5.5x multiples.

What if my club EBITDA is still ramping up?

Asset replacement value (€22k–€30k per indoor panoramic court plus amortized civil fit-out) acts as the minimum valuation floor.
