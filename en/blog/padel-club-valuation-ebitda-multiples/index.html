Padel Club Valuation: EBITDA Multiples, Lease Tenure & Asset Floors (2026)

As European padel shifts from founder-led facilities into multi-club institutional portfolios, valuations are governed strictly by normalized cash flow, warehouse lease duration, and asset replacement floors.

1. Standard EBITDA Valuation Multiples

Operating indoor padel businesses in leased warehouses typically trade on a multiple of trailing twelve-month (LTM) normalized EBITDA:

Club ProfileEBITDA MultipleKey Buyer Conditions
Mid-size Club (4–6 courts)3.0x – 3.8x EBITDA4–7y lease left, standard turf, higher owner-operator dependency.
Consolidated Indoor (7–10 courts)3.8x – 4.8x EBITDA8–15y lease tenure, >70% peak utilization, clear POS/SaaS logs.
Flagship Venue (>10 courts / Freehold)5.0x – 5.8x+ EBITDA10m+ eaves height, HVAC, proprietary F&B margin, major metro hub.

2. Normalized EBITDA Adjustments in Due Diligence

  • Market Manager Replacement Salary: Adjusting founder wages up or down to reflect hiring a full-time general club manager.
  • Deferred Capex Deductions: Discounting aged artificial turf (>4 years old) or non-LED metal-halide lighting upgrades.
  • Remaining Lease Bottleneck: If under 6 years remain on the warehouse lease without extension options, buyers drop multiples steeply; Padelgree pre-negotiates 10–15y landlord extensions prior to close.

3. Tangible Asset Replacement Floor

Even if a club is under-monetized operationally, installed 12mm panoramic courts carry €20,000–€32,000 per court replacement value plus €150k–€300k in amortized concrete floor slab, changing rooms, power upgrades, and active municipal sports permits.
