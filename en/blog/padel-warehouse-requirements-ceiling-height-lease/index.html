Padel Warehouse Requirements: Ceiling Height, Column Grid, Slab Specs & Lease Terms

Finding a compliant industrial building is the single tightest bottleneck for expanding European and UK padel operators. Here are the non-negotiable structural standards.

Structural Specifications Matrix

Architectural SpecMinimum CommercialOptimal / Competition Grade
Clear Height Under Beam/Lighting8.5m (28 ft)10.0m – 12.0m (33–39 ft)
Column-Free Module per Court11m x 21m12m x 22m+ (incl. out-of-court run)
Total Floor Area (6–10 Courts)1,800 sqm (19k sqft)2,500 – 4,500 sqm (27k–48k sqft)
Concrete Slab & FlatnessReinforced ≥150mm concrete, DIN 18202 tolerance (<3mm/3m), load ≥1,500 kg/sqm.

Essential Lease Clauses for Landlords & Tenants

  • Planning / Change-of-Use Break Clause: Conditional on obtaining municipal Class E / D2 / sports assembly consent.
  • 4–8 Month Rent-Free Fit-Out: Covers architectural permitting, concrete slab leveling, HVAC, changing facilities, and court rigging.
  • 10–15 Year Tenure + Assignment Clause: Essential to amortize €500k+ Capex and ensure smooth future M&A share transfers.
