Buying a Padel Club in Europe or the UK: Technical, Legal & Financial Due Diligence Checklist

Before signing an asset purchase or share deal for a padel business, buyers must audit four non-negotiable pillars to eliminate hidden legal and Capex liabilities.

The 4 Pillars of Padel Club Due Diligence

  • 1. Financial Reconciliation: Cross-verify Playtomic/Matchi/Syltek booking exports directly against bank merchant statements (Stripe/POS) to confirm actual cash utilization vs complimentary staff slots and test seasonal dips in summer months.
  • 2. Urban Zoning & Acoustic Permits: Verify active municipal sports operating license and noise compliance certificates near any residential boundaries.
  • 3. Landlord Assignment & Remaining Tenure: Confirm written landlord consent, rent indexation caps, and at least 8–10+ remaining years.
  • 4. Hardware & Turf Inspection: Measure LED lux levels (>400 lux uniform), check glass thickness (10mm vs 12mm), concrete slab cracks, and turf filament wear (replacing turf costs €3.5k–€4.5k/court).
