Why You Must Sell Your Padel Club 100% Off-Market (And How Blind Teasers Protect Value)

A padel club is not an empty retail shell—its enterprise value lives in recurring weekly player communities, coaching staff loyalty, and local league momentum.

What Breaks Value When a Club Leaks Publicly

  • Member & Academy Churn: Players assume maintenance or coaching continuity will suffer and switch annual packs to nearby rivals within weeks.
  • Coach & Staff Poaching: Top instructors are the club's moat; uncertainty triggers immediate approaches from competing clubs.
  • Predatory Pricing Signals: If a listing lingers publicly for months, buyers assume distress and discount offers by 25%–40%.

The Padelgree Double Opt-In Protocol

1) Blind operational teaser with zero brand or street disclosure; 2) Pre-vetting buyer proof of funds and expansion fit; 3) Mutual NDA and seller green-light before revealing club identity.
