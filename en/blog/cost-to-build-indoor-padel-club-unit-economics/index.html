Cost to Build an Indoor Padel Club: 8-Court Capex, Opex, EBITDA & IRR Breakdown

Budgeting only the €20k–€25k court hardware is the classic rookie mistake. Court steel and glass account for barely 30%–35% of turnkey indoor club build-out costs.

8-Court Indoor Turnkey Capex Breakdown (Leased Shell)

Capex Line ItemEstimated Cost Range (€)
8 Panoramic Courts (12mm glass + texturized turf + LED)€175,000 – €225,000
Concrete Slab Leveling & Civil Works€45,000 – €85,000
Locker Rooms, Showers, Lounge & F&B Fit-Out€90,000 – €160,000
HVAC / HVLS Fans, Electrical Power & Access Control€80,000 – €145,000
Engineering, Acoustic Compliance & Contingency€40,000 – €70,000
Total Turnkey Capex (8 Courts)€480,000 – €755,000

Operating Profitability (Year 2+ Stabilized)

With 60%–70% blended utilization (peak 90%+, off-peak 30%–45% backed by coaching academies and corporate leagues) plus F&B lounge and pro-shop sales, stabilized revenue reaches €525k–€815k in Spain and €900k–€1.5M+ in UK/Northern Europe, delivering 33%–38% EBITDA margins and 24%–36% IRR.
