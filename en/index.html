Frequently asked questions about padel brokerage

Why operate confidentially and off-market?

Publicly listing a padel club sale triggers member churn to competitors, staff anxiety, and discounted opportunistic bids. At Padelgree, we introduce opportunities strictly in private to vetted buyers under NDA.

What are the technical specs for a padel warehouse?

Minimum clear ceiling height of 8.5m to 11m (28–36 ft), column-free playing zones (11x21m grid per court), 1,800 to 4,500+ sqm, reinforced concrete slab, and sports/D2/commercial zoning compatibility.

How is an operating padel club valued?

The Western European market benchmark typically sits between 3.5x and 5.5x normalized EBITDA, heavily influenced by remaining warehouse lease years (ideally 8–15+), peak utilization, and court hardware condition.
